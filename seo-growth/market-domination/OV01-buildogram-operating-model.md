## 1. The distinction that governs everything

| Model | Test | Wording permitted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Directly delivered** | Buildogram staff perform, review, sign and remain responsible for corrections | "Buildogram prepares / reviews / analyses / compares / provides"; "our team performs" |
| **Buildogram-managed** | Buildogram owns the customer relationship and coordinates the complete deliverable, even when specialists participate | "Buildogram manages / coordinates the complete workflow / provides owner-side coordination / tracks and documents" |
| **Partner-coordinated** | A qualified third party performs and carries professional responsibility | "Coordinated through relevant professionals"; "delivered by an appointed construction partner"; "performed by the selected specialist" |
| **Directory / referral** | Buildogram lists or introduces only | "Listed professionals"; "Partner Network"; "explore service providers"; "request introductions" |

**Prohibited unless documented:** "our structural engineers" · "our laboratory" · "our construction crew" · "our certified surveyors" · "our authorised dealers" · "best" · "top" · "verified" · "approved" · "certified" · "guaranteed" · "trusted".

**Software does not make a capability direct.** For each proposed direct capability, confirm that a staff member performs the work, reviews it, signs or approves the deliverable, communicates recommendations, and remains responsible for corrections. If software assists but no person is accountable, it is a **tool**, not a service.

## 3. Regulated-service gates (`57-…csv`)

Thirteen services carry statutory or professional-responsibility gates: structural design · structural audit · stability certificate · NDT · soil investigation · geotechnical recommendation · land survey · drone survey · fire systems · electrical certification · lift installation · building approval · legal/property-document advice.

For each, the file records the required qualification, registration or licence, lab accreditation, authority acceptance, responsible professional, deliverable signer, Buildogram's role, permitted wording, prohibited wording and the limitation that must be stated. **Every one is publication-blocked until the responsible professional is named and evidenced.**

Two are absolute: **stability certificates** can only be issued by an appropriately registered engineer, and **drone operations** require a DGCA remote-pilot certificate held by the operator. Buildogram may coordinate both; it may claim neither.

## 4. Commercial and lead-flow model (§B5)

For every commercial capability the ledger requires: who receives the lead · qualifies it · visits site · prepares scope · prepares the quotation · contracts · invoices · collects payment · executes · supervises · provides completion documents · handles warranty · whether Buildogram earns a fee or commission · **whether that fee is disclosed**.

These answers determine CTA wording, service-page wording, schema, conversion tracking, legal disclosures, keyword eligibility and locality-page eligibility. They cannot be inferred from the website.

## 5. Service-area model (§B6) — `58-…csv`

Coverage is decided by **13 zones and corridors**, not by asking whether Buildogram "serves all Chennai". Each zone takes one of: direct staff coverage · managed partner coverage · referral coverage only · informational coverage only · not served · owner confirmation required.

**A partner's willingness to travel does not constitute coverage.** Commercial locality targeting stays blocked for every zone until a model is selected.

## 6. Research consequence

Confirming a capability updates the ledger, service taxonomy, keyword business-model compatibility, CTA permissions, schema permissions, locality eligibility, the owner register and the opportunity set.

**It does not raise any cluster above the search-evidence cap.** Capability evidence and search-demand evidence remain separate — a confirmed service with no observed queries is still a hypothesis.
